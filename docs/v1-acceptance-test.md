# V1 canvas round trip on Mac

1. Open a note that contains a block, then open a canvas beside it.
2. Drag the block by its handle onto the canvas. Confirm the card appears on the canvas and the block disappears from the note.
3. Close the canvas, close the note, and quit VaultOps. Reopen the app, note, and canvas. Confirm the block is absent from the note and present on the canvas.
4. Drag the card from the canvas into the note, between two blocks if available. Confirm it appears at the drop position and leaves the canvas. Close the canvas and note, quit VaultOps, then reopen them. Confirm the block remains in the note and is absent from the canvas.

The card's **Card actions** menu also offers **Move to open note**, which places it at the end of the open note.
